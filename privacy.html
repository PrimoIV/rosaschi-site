Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026

Rosaschi is built around a simple principle: use only what serves a clear purpose.

We do not collect information simply because we can.

Rosaschi ("we," "us," or "our") takes the same approach to personal information. We aim to collect only what is reasonably necessary to operate Rosaschi, communicate with you, and improve your experience.

This Privacy Policy explains what we collect through rosaschi.com, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

We may collect:

  • Your email address when you join the Rosaschi pre-launch or email list.
  • Your name, email address, inquiry type, and message when you contact us.
  • Information you choose to include in direct communications with Rosaschi.

Please do not send sensitive personal, medical, financial, or other confidential information unless it is necessary for your inquiry.

Information collected automatically

Our website and service providers may process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and deliver the site, such as:

  • Browser or device information
  • Pages or resources requested
  • Date and time of requests
  • Referring website or page
  • Technical information used for security and troubleshooting

We also temporarily store your first landing page and, when available, the external website that referred you to Rosaschi during your browsing session.

If you join the pre-launch list, this attribution information may be submitted with your email address so we can understand how people discover Rosaschi.

2. How We Use Information

We use personal information only for purposes related to operating Rosaschi and improving your experience, including to:

  • Manage the Rosaschi pre-launch and email list.
  • Send confirmation emails, launch information, product updates, and other communications you requested.
  • Respond to questions, support requests, wholesale inquiries, press inquiries, and other messages.
  • Understand how people discover Rosaschi.
  • Improve the website, communications, and customer experience.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of our services.
  • Prevent spam, fraud, abuse, or misuse.
  • Comply with applicable law and maintain necessary business records.

We do not use personal information for unrelated purposes without appropriate notice or consent where required.

3. Email Communications

If you join the Rosaschi email list, we may send you information about products, launches, and related updates.

We use email confirmation before a subscription is fully confirmed.

You may unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link included in our emails.

We may retain limited information necessary to record and honor your unsubscribe request.

4. Service Providers

We use a limited number of service providers to operate Rosaschi.

These include:

  • GitHub Pages for website hosting.
  • Cloudflare for website and network infrastructure.
  • Kit for email-list management and email delivery.
  • Formspree for contact-form processing.
  • Zoho Mail for business email.

These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services, such as contact details, subscriber information, form submissions, and technical request data.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect our rights, prevent abuse, or protect the security of Rosaschi and its users.

5. Selling, Advertising, and Tracking

Rosaschi does not sell personal information.

We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted advertising.

We do not use third-party advertising networks.

We do not use a third-party website analytics platform.

If these practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated and any legally required privacy choices will be provided.

6. Cookies and Browser Storage

Rosaschi does not use third-party advertising cookies.

The website uses browser session storage to temporarily retain your first landing page and, when available, the external website that referred you to Rosaschi. This helps us understand how subscribers discover the brand.

This information normally remains only for the current browser session unless you submit it when joining the pre-launch list.

Our service providers may use technologies necessary to provide hosting, security, form-processing, or email services.

7. Do Not Track

Rosaschi does not engage in cross-site behavioral advertising tracking.

Because traditional browser "Do Not Track" signals do not have a single universally adopted standard, the website does not change its operation in response to them.

If legally recognized opt-out signals become relevant to our practices, we will honor them as required.

8. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

This may include:

  • Subscriber information while you remain subscribed.
  • Limited consent or unsubscribe records where necessary.
  • Communications for as long as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain appropriate business records.
  • Technical information for periods appropriate to security, troubleshooting, and operation of the website.

We may retain information longer where required by law or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or recordkeeping.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect personal information.

No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please use care when deciding what information to send electronically.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of certain information.

You may also withdraw consent where applicable and unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.

To make a privacy request, contact hello@rosaschi.com .

We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

11. International Visitors

Rosaschi is based in the United States.

Information may be processed or stored in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate.

If privacy laws in your jurisdiction provide additional rights, we will honor those rights where applicable.

12. Children's Privacy

Rosaschi's website and products are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, contact hello@rosaschi.com .

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Rosaschi, the website, or applicable legal requirements change.

When we do, we will update the effective date above. If a change materially affects how we use personal information, we may provide additional notice where appropriate or required.
